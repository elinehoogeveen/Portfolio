What?

Building a pavilion that breathes and feeds and changes with the seasons, as it is alive. Mycelium curves offer both sound dampening and a sense of shelter, creating an oasis of rest. As the wood and mycelium decay over time, only a handful of screws will remain as a reminder of this pavilion.
